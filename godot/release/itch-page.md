**Title:** Last Stop

**Short description:** Stop your train on the platform before the rival does. Arriving first does not win.

**Genre:** Racing · **Tags:** pixel-art, trains, arcade, one-button, short, godot

**Description:**

Two trains. One station. The winner is not the one who arrives first. It is the one who comes to a stand on the platform first.

Hold SPEED UP. Watch the rival pull ahead. Judge the exact moment to brake: too early and you creep in while the rival stops, too late and you sail past the platform. You get one stop. There is no creeping forward.

- **8-station campaign** along The Coastline, from a dry dawn run to a wet night terminus
- **Three stars per station:** win, beat par, and stop on the board
- **Free race mode** with random stations, a growing streak and a rival that gets faster
- **Five liveries** to unlock in the garage
- **Ghost train:** race a see-through replay of your best run at each station
- **Rain changes everything:** a wet rail carries you about 35% further
- Pixel art, synthesized sound, and a chiptune soundtrack, all made in code
- Keyboard, gamepad, mouse and touch

**Controls:** hold W / Up to speed up. S / Down and Q set the brake lever (or keys 0 to 3). On a phone, hold SPEED UP and tap or slide the brake lever.

**Made with:** Godot 4

## Upload checklist

1. Export the **Web** preset to `godot/export/web/`.
2. Zip the contents of that folder (not the folder itself).
3. On itch.io: Kind of project **HTML**, upload the zip, tick **This file will be played in the browser**.
4. Embed options: viewport **960 x 540**, tick **Mobile friendly** and **Fullscreen button**.
5. Export Windows, Linux and macOS and upload each as a download.
6. Cover image: 630 x 500. Take a screenshot of a wet night station (Greystone) and scale it by whole numbers.
